# Mega Man 11

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Weapons received from Robot Masters, access to each individual stage, Rush Coil/Jet, 
and the Speed and Power Gears are randomized into the multiworld. 
Access to the Wily Stages is locked behind a modular goal (requiring any combination of Robot Masters defeated, weapons 
received, and receiving Gear Medals). The game is complete upon viewing the ending sequence after defeating 
Wily Machine 11.

## What Mega Man 11 items can appear in other players' worlds?
- Robot Master weapons
- Robot Master Access Codes (stage access)
- Rush Coil/Jet
- Speed/Power Gear
- Charge Buster
- Chips usually bought from Auto's Shop
- Item Schematics (for making items in Dr. Light's shop)
- 1-Ups
- E-Tanks
- Health Energy (L)
- Weapon Energy (L)
- 10 Bolts
- 100 Bolts

## What is considered a location check in Mega Man 11?
- The defeat of a Robot Master or Wily Boss
- Receiving a weapon from Dr. Light
- A gift from Dr. Light for every 2 Robot Masters defeated
- Optionally, 1-Ups, E-Tanks, and W-Tanks present within stages
- Optionally, Weapon Energy, Health Energy, Gear Recovery and Bolt pickups present within stages

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
The effects of the item will be immediately applied, such as unlocking the use of a weapon mid-stage. If the effects of 
the item cannot be fully applied (such as receiving Health Energy while at full health), the remaining are withheld 
until they can be applied.

## What is EnergyLink?
EnergyLink is an energy storage supported by certain games that is shared across all worlds in a multiworld. In Mega Man
 11, when enabled, drops from enemies are not applied directly to Mega Man and are instead deposited into the EnergyLink.
Half of the energy that would be gained is lost upon transfer to the EnergyLink. 

Energy from the EnergyLink storage can be converted into health, weapon energy, and bolts at a standard conversion rate.
A full HP bar costs 100 bolts, a full weapon bar costs 50 bolts, and bolts convert directly.
You can find the amount of bolts in the EnergyLink tab of the client, and can request energy/enable auto heal by 
clicking the corresponding buttons in the tab.

## Plando Weaknesses
Plando Weaknesses allows you to override the amount of damage a boss should take from a given weapon, ignoring prior 
weaknesses generated by strict/random weakness options. Formatting for this is as follows:
```yaml
plando_weakness:
  Block Man:
    Block: 0
    PowerBlock: 4
```
This would cause Block Man to take 4 damage from a Power Gear-boosted Block Dropper, and 0 from regular Block Dropper.

The full list of damage types are as follows:
- BusterLv0*
- BusterLv1*
- BusterLv2*
- PowerShot1*
- PowerShot2*
- FinalBuster

- PowerBlock*
- AcidBarrier
- AcidRain
- AcidBuster*
- PowerAcidBarrier
- PowerAcidRain
- PowerAcidBuster*
- PileDash*
- PileBurst
- PowerPileDash*
- PowerPileBurst
- BounceBall*
- PowerBounceBall*
- ThunderShot
- ThunderBulb*
- ThunderBurst
- PowerThunderShot
- PowerThunderBulb*
- PowerThunderBurst
- TundraStorm*
- PowerTundraStorm*

- PowerTorchShot*

Damage types marked with an asterisk (*) are those that can be considered weaknesses in weakness logic.

Note: it is possible that plando weakness will not be respected should the plando create a situation in which the game 
becomes impossible to complete. In this situation, the damage would be boosted to the minimum required to defeat the 
Robot Master.
